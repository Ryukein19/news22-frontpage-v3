# v3 build note — the visual gap (written BEFORE any code, per research §5 step 1)

Side by side: v2 entry.png (261 KB) vs top-1 "atlas22b" (jolly-stork-27, 992,370).

The gap is illustration, not information. v2's cells are TYPOGRAPHIC: a big coloured
number + headline + two lines of text on a flat colour block. Its lead "NO DEAL."
is huge type and the illustration (crossed-out pact + gavel) is small, tucked in
the top-right corner. The page reads as "clean but flat" — a spreadsheet with
colour. Top-1 puts a distinct DRAWN SCENE in every cell: a drone sky over a lit
apartment block, a plenary table with delegates and a clock, radar dishes with a
ship and a circular arrow, a ballot box, a cricket pitch, an F1 car, a basketball,
a cargo ship, power lines, an ECG, a magnifier, an aeroplane, a bar chart, a
mountain, a factory. At 300px the motifs survive; text alone does not. Top-1's
masthead is also stronger (huge serif date + a red one-line summary of the day),
it keeps a map ("WHERE IT HAPPENED") and a 21-item ALSO list, while v2 dropped
the map and has only 2 ALSO items.

v3's fix: (1) a DIFFERENT concept — a four-act timeline, not another grid — so the
page is visibly distinct at thumbnail size; (2) a drawn motif in EVERY cell (P1,
non-negotiable), with the COP31 hero as a full SCENE, not a text block; (3) the
map back as a compact strip; (4) the stat strip at the top so the day's numbers
are visible immediately. Accuracy is already better than top-1 (its lead is
factually wrong, seven numbers stale/wrong, one Saturday story drawn as Sunday);
v3 must match its craft while beating its accuracy.
