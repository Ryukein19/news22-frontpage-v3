A make.fun entry for the [news-image-20261122 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122):
one image summing up Sunday 22 November 2026.

## The concept

Not a newspaper grid — a **timeline**. The day in four acts, 00:00 → 24:00 UTC,
each with its own palette and mood:

- **Act I · Overnight** (navy) — the night the war came back: 186 drones over
  Ukraine, the Mecca missile at 01:42, Sudan, Cikande (34), Verstappen.
- **Act II · Morning** (gold) — the morning of demands: Gaza talks, Bahrain's
  UNSC demand, Nigeria (24), Iran rationing, Sparta II fire out, Bolsonaro,
  Washington pauses gun checks.
- **Act III · Afternoon** (blue/green) — the afternoon the world played: Hong
  Kong 98.79%, India 720/9, Alcaraz, the Pope's lunch, Manipur 17, Peru 152 —
  plus the brightest row on the page, five football scorelines in club colours.
- **Act IV · Evening** (crimson) — the evening the deal died: the COP31
  COLLAPSE as the climax — "NO DEAL.", the crossed-out Antalya pact and gavel,
  the only saturated-red panel on the page.

34 stories, every one dated Sunday 22 November 2026, with a HOW SURE legend,
a UTC spine, a compact world map, and a DATE CHECK naming the traps the field
gets wrong (Wembanyama Saturday, Hormuz tankers Saturday, BepiColombo Saturday,
Belgium strike Monday, El Chapo Friday, Nvidia Friday).

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies the shipped
`entry.png` — rendered from `frontpage.html` — to `/output/entry.png`.
